CoinBag privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

CoinBag is a spending tracker for mobile money, published by Lastrum ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. Contact: gold20388@gmail.com

The short version

  • Your transactions are read and stored on your phone. We do not upload them.
  • CoinBag has no accounts, no adverts and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • One optional feature, Aura online, sends information over the internet. It is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off again at any time. Versions of CoinBag without the "Use online" option send nothing at all.

Information that stays on your phone

With your permission (Android "notification access"), CoinBag reads the notifications your messaging app shows for incoming SMS, and keeps only transaction alerts from mobile money senders. Android makes notifications from all apps available to CoinBag; it ignores those from other apps. From each alert it saves the amount, date, provider, type, the other party's name and your balance, in a database on your phone. An alert that arrives while CoinBag is closed is held on your phone until you next open the app. If a message from another sender looks like a transaction, for example from a bank, CoinBag may save that sender's name, not the message, to show you in Settings.

This information, and the categories, envelopes and settings you create, never leaves your phone. We cannot see it. It is excluded from Android cloud backup.

Aura online (optional)

Aura is the assistant in CoinBag. By default it answers from your phone only. If you choose "Use online", Aura's answers are written by an AI model, and the app sends the following over an encrypted connection:

  • What you type to Aura and the replies in the current conversation.
  • Totals worked out from your data when your question needs them: spending and income totals, spending per category, and how each envelope is doing (its name, its limit or savings goal, and the amount spent or saved).
  • A random identifier created for this installation of the app, used only to apply a fair daily usage limit and to prevent abuse.

The app never sends your SMS messages, individual transactions, account balances, phone numbers, or the names of people and merchants. Please do not type such details, or other sensitive personal information, into the chat.

Who receives Aura online information

The information goes to the CoinBag AI service, which we operate, and from there to the AI model provider that writes the reply. These providers process it on our behalf to produce the answer:

  • Anthropic (Claude). See anthropic.com/legal/privacy

Your conversation with Aura is saved on your phone so it is still there when you reopen the app. "New chat" in the Aura menu deletes it.

The CoinBag AI service does not keep your conversations. It keeps short technical logs (the random identifier, time, and usage and error counts) for up to 30 days to run and protect the service. AI model providers may keep requests for a limited period under their own terms, for example to detect abuse.

We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

Reporting a reply

If you report one of Aura's replies, the text of that reply and the reason you chose are sent to us so we can review it. We keep reports for up to 12 months.

About Aura's answers

Aura's answers are generated by AI and can be wrong. They are general information and education, not professional financial, investment, tax or legal advice. We are not a licensed financial adviser.

Your choices

  • Turn Aura online on or off from the menu at the top of the Aura screen. Turning it off clears the conversation from the app.
  • Turn off notification access in Android settings to stop CoinBag reading new alerts.
  • Delete everything CoinBag has stored by clearing the app's data in Android settings or uninstalling the app.
  • To ask about information held by the CoinBag AI service, or to ask us to delete it, email us at the address above and include the date and approximate time you used Aura.

Children

CoinBag is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how CoinBag handles information, we will update this policy and its date, and ask for your agreement again where the change affects Aura online.
